Use the Creatin SEO Web adaptation as the timing master. Its visual reference was the Pinterest pin “Introducing Finqube – A Freelancers Dream,” credited on the pin to Rehbar Design Labs. The following timings describe the original Creatin adaptation.

## Visual and motion settings

- Default to 1080 × 1920 at 30 fps. Keep essential text about 8% inside the left and right edges. Let peripheral chips bleed offscreen.
- Use cream `#FBFBF2`, near-black green `#10271D`, forest `#0B3328`, and lime `#BCF75D`. Retain the light/dark rhythm when using another brand accent. Keep dashboard detail subordinate to the headline.
- Set the main message in a bold sans serif. Use a regular face for descriptions and a compact monospaced face for a few interface labels. Aim for two short lines and one visual focus per beat.
- Animate text with a fast start and a slow stop. The template uses quartic ease-out, a 0.65 s entrance and about 0.1 s between lines. Cards and the brand tile use a damped spring. Alternate horizontal wipes, upward reveals, and short pushes over about 0.32 s.
- Use the bundled original stereo score for the default cut. Leave enough reading time with the audio muted. Keep a supplied voice-over or music request in the new edit's brief.

## 15-second edit

Use five readable extracts from the long sequence. The shot starts are 0.0, 3.4, 5.8, 8.3, and 11.6 seconds. Keep the hook, mark, concrete example, product demo, and closing invitation. Show one benefit in the headline. Save the three-card detail and desktop-to-mobile transformation for the long cut.

## Brief fields

Use `intro1/intro2` for the setup and `hook/hook2` for the question. The `s1`, `s3`, `s4`, `s5`, `s6`, and `s7` pairs supply the two headline lines for their scenes. Use `query` and `chip1–3` for the discovery step, `sample/result/resultBody/resultLink` for its result, and `hero1/hero2/shopCTA` for the desktop and phone. The three detail cards show the result title, the joined hero lines, and `shopLink`. Set `route1–3` to the customer's steps and `cta` to the single closing action. Use `category`, `galleryTitle`, and `galleryMeta` for details visible inside the mockup.
